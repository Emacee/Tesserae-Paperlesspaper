# Waste, next collection (`waste_next`)

Which bin goes out next, and on what day, with an icon. Needs your postcode and
house number under **Settings › Widgets › Mijn Afvalwijzer** (the
`afvalwijzer_core` plugin).

## Two ways to show it

- **A round tile** (*Show as: A round tile*), in the style of the Deye and car
  rings: a ring that fills as the day comes closer (empty two weeks ahead, full
  on the day), the bin inside in the colour of its stream, its name under it and
  the day (**Today** and **Tomorrow** in the accent colour, else *Friday 4 dec*
  and *in 3 days*). Two bins on one day (say residual waste and plastic) show
  side by side. It fits a small cell next to the battery rings.
- **A list** (*Show as: A list*): the next collections (up to eight), one row
  per day, with the icons of what goes out.

Either way it can be limited to some types (*Only these types*, for instance
`gft,restafval`).

## In the agenda

To see the collections between your appointments instead, switch on *Waste
collection* in the **Agenda, with travel time** widget: every collection is an
item on its day, with the same icon.

## Icons and colours

A wheelie bin in the colour of the stream (paper blue, garden and food green,
plastic and cans ochre, residual waste black, glass and Christmas trees moss,
textiles purple), with a small sign on it: lines for paper, a leaf, a bottle, a
shirt, a tree. The colours are the theme's, flat, so they stay clean on e-paper.
